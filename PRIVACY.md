# Privacy Policy for FRIDAY 2.0

**Effective Date:** October 2026

FRIDAY 2.0 ("the Application") is a personal, locally-hosted desktop AI assistant. 

## Data Collection and Usage
- **Local Execution:** The Application runs locally on your device. It does not possess a centralized database or backend server that collects, stores, or processes your personal data.
- **Pinterest Integration:** If you choose to connect your Pinterest account, the Application communicates directly with the official Pinterest API from your local machine. 
- **OAuth Tokens:** The Application securely encrypts and stores your Pinterest access and refresh tokens locally on your device using native Windows data protection (DPAPI). These tokens are never transmitted to any third-party servers other than Pinterest itself for authentication purposes.
- **Read-Only Access:** The Application requests read-only scopes (`pins:read`, `boards:read`, `user_accounts:read`) to display your saved pins and boards natively within the local user interface. 

## Data Sharing
The Application does not sell, rent, or share your personal data or Pinterest data with any third parties. All API requests are made directly between your local device and the respective service providers (e.g., Pinterest).

## User Control
You can revoke the Application's access to your Pinterest account at any time by:
1. Clicking "Disconnect" within the FRIDAY 2.0 Control Center.
2. Revoking access directly from your Pinterest account settings.

## Changes to this Policy
As a personal development project, this privacy policy may be updated occasionally. All changes will be reflected in this file.
